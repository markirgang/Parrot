⚙️ L298N LOGIC TRUTH TABLE
• Beak open (Channel 1 forward): ENA = PWM, IN1 = HIGH, IN2 = LOW → beak opens in speech rhythm.
• Eyes blink (Channel 1 reverse): ENA = PWM 65%, IN1 = LOW, IN2 = HIGH → eye mechanism blinks.
• Channel 1 stop: ENA = 0, IN1 = LOW, IN2 = LOW → beak and eyes rest.
• Wings flap (Channel 2 forward): ENB = PWM 90%, IN3 = HIGH, IN4 = LOW → wings apply constant forward polarity.
• Channel 2 stop: ENB = 0, IN3 = LOW, IN4 = LOW.
💡 SPOTLIGHT & STAGE BEHAVIOUR
• Dancing: the IRLZ44N gate is PWM-flashed, the 2-DOF spotlight sweeps with the beat and the stage servo sweeps rapidly.
• Talking: the LED shines steadily from the center position and breathes slowly; the stage sweeps slowly.
• Idle: the spotlight rests dim at center and the stage makes an occasional slow sweep.
• Power: use a separate 5-6V servo supply with a common ground. Never power the servos from the Pi 5 5V pin.
📖 Gemmy AI Parrot Operating Manual
Assembly, wiring, Raspberry Pi 5 setup, AI conversation, local/remote audio and music performance guide.
1. 🦜 MECHANICAL ASSEMBLY
1. Mount the large Gemmy toy parrot on the stage perch and balance it over the turntable servo.
2. Couple the L298N channel 1 output to the mouth / eye mechanism so forward polarity opens the beak and reverse polarity blinks the eyes.
3. Couple channel 2 to the wing linkage so constant forward polarity flaps both wings.
4. Fit the 2-DOF spotlight on its pan/tilt bracket and aim it at the parrot.
5. Confirm the stage servo can rotate freely through 30-150° without straining the wiring.
2. ⚡ POWER & WIRING
1. Power the L298N motor rail from a 12V supply and connect the Pi 5 through its own 5V/5A USB-C supply.
2. Common ground is critical: join the Pi GND, L298N GND, IRLZ44N source, servo supply GND and 12V supply GND.
3. Wire GPIO 12 through a 220Ω resistor to the IRLZ44N gate and add a 10kΩ pull-down to GND.
4. Power the spotlight LED from 12V through the MOSFET drain/source path.
5. Use a separate 5-6V 5A supply for the three servos.
3. 💻 SOFTWARE STARTUP
1. Install dependencies: pip install google-genai opencv-python numpy gTTS reportlab gpiozero lgpio.
2. Start the server: PORT=1790 HOST=0.0.0.0 python3 server.py.
3. Open http://<raspberry-pi-ip>:1790 from the Pi or any remote computer.
4. Add a Gemini API key in Hardware & Settings or export GEMINI_API_KEY before starting the server.
4. 🎙️ VOICE & AUDIO ROUTING
1. The browser microphone can come from a remote laptop, phone or the webcam on the Pi.
2. Use the mic VU meter and sensitivity slider to tune voice activity detection.
3. Choose a remote speaker to hear Coco on the browser device anywhere in the world, or a local Pi speaker to play through the Raspberry Pi.
4. The master volume slider applies to browser playback and to local server playback.
5. 🎵 MUSIC & TROUBLESHOOTING
• Import MP3/WAV/OGG files by drag-and-drop or direct URL. Select Play & Dance to start the show.
• The analyser pulses the beak, flaps the wings, flashes the spotlight and sweeps the stage to the beat.
• No movement? Check the GPIO status card and verify the common ground bus.
• No audio on the Pi? Select a local speaker in Settings and confirm pw-play or aplay works.
• No AI reply? Confirm the Gemini key and internet connection.
